The 2 PM Attic Crisis

You're under a unit in a customer's attic at 2 PM on a Tuesday in July. Your phone rings. You can't answer. The homeowner has a broken air conditioner and it's 95 degrees outside. They hang up without leaving a message and immediately call the next contractor on their Google search results.

That scenario plays out dozens of times a month for the typical HVAC business. Meanwhile, a customer from last week hasn't left a Google review. An estimate you sent went unanswered. A maintenance contract is due for renewal and no one has followed up. None of this is happening because you don't care - it's happening because you're running a business and there aren't enough hours in the day.

check_circle

The Linchpin Solution

Linchpin catches the lead before they go cold. When a call is missed, the system texts the caller back in under a minute and asks what they need - AC, heating, or an emergency. That question does real work: it pulls the caller into a conversation and keeps them engaged with you instead of dialing the next company on Google while they wait. Their reply routes straight to you, so you call back already knowing the job instead of dialing blind. You get a priority callback alert, and if you run cloud-based scheduling software, every lead is logged straight into your CRM or FSM. Additionally, Linchpin offers other systems that keep the follow-up moving by chasing unsold estimates, collecting Google reviews, and handling the small touches that usually get buried when you’re running calls, crews, and customers all day.

Unsold Estimate Follow-Up

expand_more

When an estimate hasn’t converted into a booked job within 5–7 days, the system automatically triggers an SMS and email follow-up sequence. It helps recover revenue from diagnostic time you already invested instead of letting good estimates die in the inbox.

Maintenance Contract Renewal Sequences

expand_more

The system checks your FSM for maintenance contracts expiring in 30, 60, and 90 days, then sends a systematic SMS and email renewal sequence at each interval. What used to live in a spreadsheet, or in someone’s memory, becomes a reliable recurring revenue protection system.

Review Collection Automation

expand_more

When a job is marked complete in your FSM, the system waits 2–4 hours and sends the customer an SMS with a direct Google review link. If no review comes in, it follows up by email after 48 hours. This helps compound your Google Business Profile ranking over time without your team having to remember every ask.

Appointment Reminder Sequences

expand_more

When an appointment is scheduled, the system sends an immediate booking confirmation, followed by a 24-hour reminder and a 2-hour reminder before arrival. Customers know you’re coming, your team gets fewer “are you still on the way?” calls, and no-shows drop in a measurable way.

Dormant Customer Reactivation

expand_more

The system finds customers in your FSM who haven’t booked service in 12–18 months and sends a seasonal SMS and email campaign to bring them back. It turns your old customer database into booked jobs without manual list pulling or one-off outreach.

Seasonal Campaign Sequences

expand_more

Customer lists are filtered by last service type, equipment age, service area, or other FSM data, then used for pre-season tune-up campaigns, post-storm inspection offers, and equipment replacement conversations. It creates bookings that usually never happen because nobody has time to run the outreach.

New Customer Onboarding Sequence

expand_more

When a first job is booked, the system sends a welcome SMS with the technician name and arrival window, plus a welcome email with pre-appointment instructions. After the job is complete, the customer automatically flows into the review collection sequence.

Technician Dispatch & Job Status

expand_more

When a technician is marked en route, the customer automatically gets an SMS with the ETA. This cuts down the “where is my technician?” calls that drain dispatcher time and keeps customers informed without extra manual updates.

Payment Reminder & Invoice Follow-Up

expand_more

When an invoice stays unpaid after a defined number of days, the system sends an automated SMS and email reminder sequence. It reduces outstanding receivables without the owner or office team having to make awkward manual collection calls.

Operational Workflow Automation

expand_more

Internal routing, job assignments, alerts, invoicing triggers, QuickBooks sync, and other handoffs are automated around your actual process. This is the connective tissue that keeps the operation moving without everything depending on manual follow-through.

Reputation Monitoring and Response Alerts

expand_more

When a new review is detected, the system alerts the owner and can draft a response suggestion. You stay on top of your online reputation without manually checking Google every day or letting reviews sit unanswered.

Employee and Technician Onboarding Automation

expand_more

When a new hire starts, the system triggers a structured onboarding checklist, document collection sequence, and training milestone reminders. It reduces the admin load of hiring and helps new technicians move through the process without constant manual chasing.
